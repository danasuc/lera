Use of English Part 4 / Transformations
Task 3.2: Key Word Transformation (2 to 5 words)
Заполни пропуск от 2 до 5 слов, включая выделенное капсом слово (не меняя его форму):
  1. We won't finish the essay on time without your advice.
    HELP
    Unless __________________________________________, we won't finish the essay on time. →
  2. Julio will not get into that college because his marks are low.
    HAD
    If Julio __________________________________________, he would get into that college. →
  3. You can only borrow books if you have a library card.
    UNLESS
    You cannot borrow books __________________________________________ a library card. →
